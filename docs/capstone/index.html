What we build

Every yard asks for a different fence. These are the six kinds of work we take on, from a new privacy run to a single leaning post.

  • Wood privacy fences

    Custom cedar, pine and pressure-treated wood privacy fences designed for longevity.

  • Aluminum and ornamental iron

    Elegant, rust-free decorative metal fencing and pool enclosures with lifetime durability.

  • Vinyl and composite

    Low-maintenance vinyl fencing that resists fading, rotting, warping and harsh weather.

  • Chain link and security

    Durable, cost-effective residential and commercial security fencing with privacy slats.

  • Custom gates and automation

    Driveway and pedestrian gates with heavy-duty hinges, keypad access and automatic openers.

  • Repair and post replacement

    Prompt storm damage repair, leaning post reinforcement and broken picket replacement.
