Questions

The things people ask before they say yes.

Straight answers, no fine print. If yours isn't here, ask.

How long does it take?

Most sites are live within two weeks of our first call, and you see a first design within a few days. The main thing that slows it down is waiting on your input, so we keep what we ask for short.

Is $499 really the whole price?

Yes, for everything listed above. The only things billed separately are your domain name and hosting, which those providers charge you directly at their normal rates, and any optional add-on you choose to ask for. There is no second invoice from us.

When do I pay?

One payment of $499, no subscriptions. Exactly when it's due is spelled out in the one-page agreement before any work starts.

What if I already have a site?

We'll look at it together. Sometimes there's good material to keep, like photos, reviews, and your menu. We rebuild the rest from scratch, same flat price, and point your existing domain at the new site so nothing breaks.

Do I need to write anything?

No. You talk, we write. We'll ask about your business, your customers, and what you want people to do when they land on the site. Then we draft the words and you tell us what to fix.

What about photos and a logo?

Bring what you have. Phone photos of your shop, your team, and your work beat stock photos every time. If you have none, we'll tell you exactly which three to take. No logo? We'll set your name in type that suits you, so the site never looks unfinished.

What about my domain and hosting?

We set both up as part of the $499. If you already own a domain, we connect it. If not, we help you buy one in your name, so it's yours, not ours. Hosting is set up in an account you control. The domain and hosting themselves are billed by those providers directly to you at their normal rates; we don't mark them up, and nothing goes to Digital Doorway Marketing after the $499.

Can I update it myself later?

Yes. Before launch we walk you through changing hours, prices, photos, and text. If you'd rather never touch it, ongoing updates are an optional add-on.

What's not included in $499?

Things that are genuinely extra: online booking systems, e-commerce with a shopping cart, large photo galleries, and ongoing monthly updates. We'll tell you plainly if something you want falls outside, before any work starts.

Is there a contract?

A one-page agreement that says what we'll build, what you'll pay, and that you own everything when it's done. No monthly commitment, no auto-renewals, nothing to cancel.

Still wondering something? Ask us. Plain answer, no pitch.
